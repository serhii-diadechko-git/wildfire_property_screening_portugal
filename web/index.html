How to use this map

Click a cell to see its 2026 comparative estimate and descriptive 1, 3, and 5 km context summaries. The map helps narrow broad areas for further research; it is not a property-level forecast, safety guarantee, insurance quote, or buy/do-not-buy recommendation.

The estimate is target-free: 2026 observed burned-area data are not yet available. The displayed score is the estimated share of the cell’s land area that may burn, not a probability.

Project estimate: ICNF burned-area history, Copernicus CLC and DEM, ERA5-Land, and DGT CAOP. Basemap: © OpenStreetMap contributors.
